# Validation handoff

The ranking is upstream of validation. Validation is where the real cost lives.

## Four questions every candidate has to clear

1. Does perturbing it reduce disease burden in a real assay?
2. Does the effect generalise across strains or pathogen classes?
3. Is the yield or growth penalty acceptable?
4. Is there a realistic route to deployment in this crop?

Most plausible looking targets fail one of the inner two.

## Minimum package per target

- infection assay in an established model
- effect size, not just present or absent
- parallel growth and fitness readout
- pre committed go/no-go thresholds (numbers, not adjectives)

## What the partner needs from us

- the row level evidence behind the ranking, not just the final score
- the assumptions behind each weight in the rubric
- the failure modes we are worried about
- candidate edits with at least one fallback

## Partner types worth talking to first

- plant pathology labs already running infection assays in the relevant model
- crop genetics groups with established CRISPR or base editing pipelines in the target crop
- public breeding programmes with regulatory experience for edited crops in the target geography

The right first conversation is rarely the most senior person. It is the postdoc who already has the assay running.
